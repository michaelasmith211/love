Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

At lovecalc.click, accessible from https://lovecalc.click, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors.

1. Zero Data Collection on Names and Dates

Unlike many online quiz platforms, lovecalc.click does not store, collect, or transmit any names, partner details, birthdates, or quiz responses entered into our calculator. All mathematical calculations, animations, and image rendering take place strictly on your device using client-side JavaScript.

2. Analytics & Performance

We use Google Analytics (GA4) with IP anonymization to understand aggregate website performance (e.g., page views, general device categories, and popular calculator features). No personal names, partner information, or calculation inputs are ever tracked or shared with Google Analytics.

3. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies to track your behavior across other websites. A simple local storage variable is used only to remember if you preferred sound effects on or off.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy, please reach out to us at support@lovecalc.click.
