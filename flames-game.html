Rules & Meaning of FLAMES

What Does FLAMES Stand For?

The FLAMES test is one of the most famous and beloved relationship games in the world. It works through a step-by-step elimination technique:

  1. Both names are written down, and all identical matching letters are eliminated.
  2. The total count of all remaining uncancelled letters is calculated.
  3. Counting cycles through the letters F-L-A-M-E-S until only one letter remains.
