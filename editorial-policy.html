Editorial Policy & Review Standards

Last Reviewed: September 13, 2026

At lovecalc.click, we adhere to strict editorial standards to ensure our articles, relationship guides, and interactive tools are accurate, constructive, and uplifting.

1. Evidence-Based Relationship Content

Our educational guides cite verified relationship studies and clinical literature, including the works of Dr. John Gottman, Dr. Helen Fisher, and Dr. Gary Chapman. We ensure that psychological concepts (such as attachment theory and love languages) are accurately depicted.

2. Algorithm Consistency & No Artificial Inflation

We do not alter calculation results for commercial incentives or ad placements. Our calculations remain strictly deterministic based on our published mathematical methodology.

3. Fact-Checking & Review Process

All editorial content is reviewed by editors with backgrounds in interpersonal communication and behavioral psychology prior to publication.

4. Corrections Policy

If you identify a factual inaccuracy, mathematical discrepancy, or typo in any of our articles, please notify us at editorial@lovecalc.click. Corrections are reviewed and published within 48 hours.
